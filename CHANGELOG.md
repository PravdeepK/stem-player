# What's new in Stem Player

Newest first. Each version's section becomes its release notes on GitHub, which is the "What's new" link the installer shows after an update.

## 0.1.1

- **Your first song no longer looks like it starts over.** The one-time stem engine download, the first download of each quality's model and the separation itself each get their own label and progress bar. Before, they shared one bar that reached 100% and then began again.
- **Clearer when there's no internet.** If a model can't download, Stem Player says so instead of blaming the GPU. Songs you've already made still open offline.
- **A ? button** in the bottom-right corner to report a bug or ask for a feature. No GitHub account needed.
- **Trackpad status in the menu.** It shows whether your trackpad can play taps, with a shortcut to turn them on in System Settings if they're off.
- **The level meter can be turned off** in the menu.
- **A bigger app icon.**

## 0.1.0

The first release.

- Split any song into vocals, drums, bass and instrumental, right on your Mac.
- Mix them on a 3D stem player: slide a groove for volume, tap to mute, hold to hear a stem alone.
- Fast or Best quality, on the GPU or the CPU.
- Trackpad taps on the drums.
- Recent songs reopen instantly, and Export saves the stems anywhere you like.
